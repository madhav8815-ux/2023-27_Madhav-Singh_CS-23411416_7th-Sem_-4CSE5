## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this job simulation were to:

- Perform exploratory data analysis and risk profiling.
- Identify factors associated with customer delinquency.
- Develop a predictive modeling strategy for delinquency risk.
- Translate analytical findings into business recommendations.
- Design an AI-driven collections strategy.
- Incorporate responsible AI, fairness, explainability and human oversight.

---

## 📂 Tasks Completed

### Task 1 – Exploratory Data Analysis & Risk Profiling

The first task focused on understanding the quality and characteristics of the financial dataset.

Key areas covered:

- Missing and inconsistent records
- Data anomalies and outliers
- Credit utilization analysis
- Debt-to-income (DTI) analysis
- Historical delinquency patterns
- Recent credit inquiry behavior
- Data preprocessing and imputation strategies

Important risk indicators identified included high revolving credit utilization, recurring late payments, high DTI ratios and recent credit-inquiry velocity.

---

### Task 2 – Predictive Delinquency Modeling

The second task developed a predictive modeling plan for identifying customers at risk of delinquency.

The proposed architecture uses:

- **XGBoost / LightGBM**
- **Logistic Regression baseline**
- **SHAP explainability**

Key predictive features included:

- Revolving credit utilization
- Historical missed payments
- Debt-to-income ratio
- Recent credit inquiries
- Employment status and income stability

The proposed workflow follows:

**Data Preprocessing → Feature Engineering → Model Inference → Risk Classification → SHAP Explanation**

Customers can subsequently be categorized into:

- Low Risk
- Medium Risk
- High Risk

---

### Task 3 – AI-Driven Collections Strategy

The third task translated predictive insights into a strategic business solution.

The strategy focuses on moving from reactive collections to **proactive early intervention**.

Key recommendations include:

- Early-warning identification
- Automated SMS/App notifications
- Flexible payment rescheduling
- Supportive repayment options
- Risk-based customer engagement
- Monitoring against a holdout control group

A key business objective proposed in the strategy is an **18% reduction in 30-to-90-day roll rates** for the high-utilization customer segment.

---

### Task 4 – Autonomous & Responsible AI-Driven Collections Strategy

The final task developed an end-to-end AI-driven collections architecture.

The architecture consists of:

**1. Data Ingestion**
- Transaction information
- Account balances
- Credit utilization
- Delinquency history
- Credit inquiry velocity

**2. Predictive Decision Engine**
- XGBoost-based scoring
- Risk probability
- SHAP reason codes
- Policy and eligibility thresholds

**3. Intelligent Action**
- SMS/App alerts
- Flexible payment rescheduling
- Supportive repayment offers
- Escalation of complex cases

**4. Learning Loop**
- Repayment response tracking
- PSI monitoring
- Roll-rate measurement
- Model recalibration

## 🤖 Agentic AI & Human Oversight

The proposed system separates routine automated activities from sensitive decisions.

### Automated Activities

- Digital notifications
- Standard payment assistance
- AI-guided payment proposals
- Routine conversational interactions
- Continuous credit-health monitoring

### Human Oversight

Human review is retained for:

- Financial hardship cases
- Complex restructuring
- Customer disputes
- Fraud allegations
- Negotiated settlements
- External agency referrals
- Legal proceedings
- Credit-line freezing or cancellation

The core principle is:

> **Automate routine, high-volume activities while reserving human judgment for sensitive decisions.**

---

## 🛡️ Responsible AI

The proposed solution incorporates several responsible-AI safeguards:

- Removal of protected attributes and geographical proxies
- Disparate Impact monitoring
- 4/5ths (80%) rule
- SHAP-based explanations
- Human-in-the-loop decision making
- Right-to-appeal mechanism
- Protection against excessive automated intervention
- Communication and consent safeguards
- Model drift monitoring
